About NoTime2Die

Not a medical device. Research prototype for NASA Space Apps 2026.

Problem

As crews travel farther from Earth, light-time delay and blackouts break the classic "call the flight surgeon" loop. NoTime2Die is a health copilot that gathers indicators, scores risk with explainable thresholds and sources, and changes how much it hands to the crew as the comms delay grows.

RIDGE hazards

Modules are tagged to the five HRP RIDGE hazards: Radiation (R), Isolation (I), Distance from Earth (D), Gravity fields (G), and closed Environments (E). See nasa.gov/hrp/hazards.

How scoring works

  1. Each health module reads simulated vitals / self-reports / PVT.
  2. It returns green / amber / red with plain-English reasons and evidence ids.
  3. Triage combines the risk with the current one-way delay (never hardcoded) to pick EARTH GUIDED, CREW LED, or FULL AUTONOMY.
  4. If time-to-act is shorter than round trip, crew-led action is forced first.

Privacy principles

  • Astronaut owns their data.
  • Local first — no backend, no accounts, no cloud sync in this prototype.
  • Share by choice (journal opt-in for ground packet).
  • No surveillance score — modules explain themselves; no hidden ranking of crew.

Disclaimer

Simulated crew data only. Not for clinical use. Thresholds marked demo assumption are design choices until the team attaches a source. No NASA logos or official branding.
